# Odd Archive

Odd Archive is a YouTube channel about unsolved mysteries, true crime, history and money, told in a way that is serious but never boring.

Watch the channel: https://www.youtube.com/@OddArchiveCases

Odd Archive Uploader is a private tool used only by the channel owner to publish videos to the Odd Archive YouTube channel through the YouTube Data API.

Privacy policy: https://david-918.github.io/odd-archive/privacy
